01 / Identity

One identity.
Every touchpoint.

Selected direction: Humanist. Persimmon, ink and ivory. The approved symbol and outlined lettering remain fixed; this kit defines everything around them.

Outlined master artwork · minimum lockup width 150px · clear space ≥ half the symbol width

Character

Direct, attentive, accountable. Large editorial headlines meet carefully structured operational evidence.

Brand promise

Resolve missed home-care visits with a dispatcher-approved exception desk. Present this as a proposed workflow, with concept status visible.

Keep the shape intact

No stretching, recolored letters, enclosing badges, gradients or shadows on the logo. Use the symbol alone below 150px.

Background rhythm

10 / Motion rules

Choreograph.
Then let it settle.

Motion is paced in three layers: a slow environment, a purposeful product sequence, and crisp interaction feedback. Reading surfaces and controls remain stable.

Maximum two active focal movements in a scene. Finish each sequence with a readable hold.

Animate transforms and opacity where possible. Avoid large blur filters, continuous full-screen repaints and physics on text.

Pause loops outside the viewport and when the document is hidden. A global motion control is always reachable.

Respect operating-system reduced motion. Keep every fact, number, decision and source available in a complete still state.

Motion is never evidence.

Do not animate success percentages, care outcomes, billability or savings. A moving meter shows named synthetic hours, not measured business performance.

Scrolling never approves a replacement, corrects EVV evidence, sends a message or writes to another system.

11 / Content & IA

The story stays
specific to CareKiln.

The original IA still supplies the information coverage. This visual system replaces its earlier unselected palette and identity proposal. All five offers remain intact.

Speak to operations.

Buyer: Medicaid-funded agency COO or staffing director. Operator: dispatcher. Explain the exception, constraints, evidence and accountable next action.

Show the boundary.

“Proposed module”, “DEMO — SYNTHETIC DATA”, “Integration discovery required”, “Draft — unsent”. No customer logos, production integrations or healthcare compliance claims.

Ask for a replay.

Primary CTA: Request a scoped pilot. First step: an exception-workflow replay. Hypothetical price: $650/branch/month + $3,000 state/EVV setup. No checkout.
